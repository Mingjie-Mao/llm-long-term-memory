# The v4.2 binary gate is underpowered — 2026-09-12

Found before any provider call, while reviewing the frozen v4.2 package. No threshold is
moved and no archived result is reopened; a second reading is registered alongside the
existing gate.

## The finding

The registered promotion rule is `net >= +6` **and** `exact cluster p <= 0.05` **and**
mechanism observed **and** safety direction passes, read on 30 count probes with three
repeats.

The conjunction is far stricter than `net >= +6` reads. Because the cluster sign-flip test
is essentially a 30-probe sign test here — 29 namespaces for 30 probes, so clustering is
almost a no-op — clearing `p <= 0.05` at net +6 requires close to zero losses:

| result | net | cluster p | promotes |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| 6W-0L | +6 | 0.031 | **yes** |
| 7W-1L | +6 | 0.070 | no |
| 8W-2L | +6 | 0.109 | no |
| 13W-5L | +8 | 0.096 | no |

Simulated against the corrected pair churn (27/142 binary correctness changes, reduced by
three-repeat majority voting):

| true gain | churn 0.107 | churn 0.250 |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 6 probes | **31%** | 15% |
| 8 probes | 56% | 27% |
| 10 probes | 75% | 40% |
| 15 probes | 97% | 74% |

**So the most likely outcome of spending ~541 requests is "inconclusive", even if the
candidate works exactly as designed.** That is the mirror image of the defect
`gate-protocol.md` was written to prevent: not a gate too fine to read, but one too strict
to pass. The point of the protocol is to make a paid run worth paying for, and a design
with 31% power at its own registered effect size does not meet that bar.

## Why not simply relax the gate

Because moving a registered threshold after seeing nothing is still moving a registered
threshold, and the conservatism is not wrong — it is just expensive. The gate stays.

## What was added instead

A second registered reading on the quantity the candidate actually changes: **gold facts
that were in the retrieved context and were never named.**

Thirty binary outcomes is a coarse ruler. A count of facts is a fine one, and on the runs
already paid for it reads control 130, `v4.0-flat` 45, `v4.0-flat2` 45, `v4.1-scan` 44.

The two same-configuration runs agreeing exactly at the total is partly luck — 11 of 30
probes move and the movements cancel. The honest figure is the paired per-probe spread:

| | |
|---|---:|
| paired per-probe difference, flat vs flat2 | mean 0.000, sd 0.830 |
| standard error of the 30-probe total | **4.6 facts** |
| two-sigma band on the total | **±9** |
| control → v4 step | 85 facts ≈ **19 sigma** |

Registered threshold: the candidate must leave at least **10 fewer** facts unnamed. That
is a 22% reduction in the 45 that remain — resolvable, where the binary gate cannot
reliably resolve its own registered effect.

**It decides nothing.** The binary gate remains the only promotion criterion. This reading
is evidence about the mechanism. A candidate that clears it and fails the gate has shown
that it does what it claims and not that it is worth promoting — and that is the most
likely, and still reportable, outcome.

**What it inherits.** Membership is decided by token overlap against the probe's
SQL-derived gold memories, so the absolute level carries the matcher's error. The matcher
is byte-identical across arms and the reading is paired, so a difference survives a biased
absolute level. Only the difference is registered.

## Consequence for the freeze

`tools/v42_protocol.py` and the addendum are bound by the freeze manifest, so
`results/frozen/v4.2-development-20260912/` correctly refuses to verify after this change.
That refusal is the mechanism working. It is retired with `SUPERSEDED.md`, its rehearsal
evidence kept, and `results/frozen/v4.2-development-20260912b/` prepared and rehearsed in
its place: 404/404 rows, 404 fake attempts, zero provider calls, Gate 0 PASS, 90/90
candidate count answers through the citation path, and the new reading computed end to end.
